Hours & Visiting

6 Questions · FAQPage Schema On Each

What Are The Opening Hours At THRöW Social DC?

Thursday 7PM–2AM · Friday 7PM–3AM · Saturday 12PM–3AM · Sunday 12PM–2AM · Monday To Wednesday Closed.

Do I Need A Reservation To Visit THRöW Social DC?

No — Walk-Ins Are Always Welcome On A First-Come, First-Served Basis. Reservations Guarantee A Table Or Cabana And Are Recommended On Friday And Saturday Nights As They Fill Up Early.

What Is The Age Policy At THRöW Social DC?

21+ After 9PM Daily. All Ages Are Welcome Before 9PM With Valid ID. Valid Government-Issued Photo ID Required For Entry.

Is There A Dress Code At THRöW Social DC?

Smart Casual Is Recommended On Friday And Saturday Nights. No Athletic Wear, Overly Casual Attire, Or Sportswear. Management Reserves The Right To Refuse Entry.

Is The Rooftop Open In Winter?

Yes — The Rooftop Has Roll-Up Doors Creating An Indoor And Outdoor Feel. THRöW Social DC Is Open Year Round Thu Through Sun Regardless Of Season.

Is There A Sunday Brunch At THRöW Social DC?

Yes — The Sunday Brunch Social Runs Every Sunday From 12PM. No Cover Charge. Brunch Menu, Cocktails, Bar Games, And Rooftop Access. Walk-Ins Welcome Or Reserve A Table In Advance.

Reservations & Booking

6 Questions

How Do I Make A Reservation At THRöW Social DC?

All Reservations Are Made Via Tock. Click Reserve Now, Select Your Table Or Cabana Type, Choose Your Date And Time, And Complete The Booking In Under 2 Minutes.

What Is The Reservation Fee At THRöW Social DC?

All Table And Cabana Reservations Include A $100 Venue Fee Plus A Food And Beverage Minimum Of $400–$600 Depending On Table Type And Night.

What Are The Table Types Available At THRöW Social DC?

Three Table Types: Stage Front (Best Views Of The Stage, Up To 8 Guests, $100 + $450–600 Min), Interior (Great Atmosphere, Up To 8 Guests, $100 + $400–500 Min), And Double (Large Groups, Up To 20 Guests, Contact For Pricing).

Can I Cancel Or Modify My Reservation?

Cancellation And Modification Policies Are Managed Through Tock At The Time Of Booking. Contact Guests@Throwsocialdc.Com For Assistance With An Existing Reservation.

How Far In Advance Should I Book A Table?

We Recommend Booking 1–2 Weeks In Advance For Friday And Saturday Nights. Stage Front Tables Sell Out Fastest, Especially On GoGo Fridays. Thursday And Sunday Bookings Are Generally Easier To Secure With Shorter Notice.

Can I Make A Reservation For A Large Group?

Yes — Double Tables Accommodate Up To 20 Guests. For Groups Larger Than 20 Or Full Private Event Enquiries, Contact Guests@Throwsocialdc.Com Or Use The Private Events Enquiry Form.

Cover Charges

4 Questions

What Is The Cover Charge At THRöW Social DC?

Friday: $20 (8PM–10PM) And $30 (10PM–Close). Saturday: $20 (10PM–Close). Thursday: No Cover. Sunday: No Cover. Cover Charges May Vary For Special Events.

Do I Still Pay Cover If I Have A Table Reservation?

Table And Cabana Reservations Include A $100 Venue Fee Which Covers Entry For Your Group. Cover Charges At The Door Are For Walk-In Guests Without A Reservation.

Is There A Cover Charge For Sunday Brunch?

No — The Sunday Brunch Social Has No Cover Charge. Walk In Free From 12PM.

Can Cover Charges Change For Special Events?

Yes — Special Events May Have Different Cover Charges. Check The Events Calendar Or Follow @Throwsocialdc On Instagram For Weekly Updates.

VIP Cabanas

5 Questions

How Much Does A VIP Cabana Cost At THRöW Social DC?

All Cabanas Have A $100 Venue Fee Plus A Food And Beverage Minimum Of $400–$600 Depending On Type And Night. The 3-Hour Experience Includes Complimentary Welcome Shots For Guests 21+.

What Is Included In A Cabana Booking?

Every Cabana Includes A Furnished Private Space, Complimentary Welcome Shots (21+), Dedicated Server, And 3 Hours Of VIP Experience. Optional Add-Ons: Welcome Bottle, Balloon Arch, Polaroid Camera, And Custom Cups.

Can I Book A Cabana For A Birthday Party?

Yes — Book Within 6 Days Of Your Birthday For A Free Birthday Package: Manager Shoutout On The 20-Ft Stage, Birthday Dessert, Sparkler, And Welcome Shots For The Group. Just Mention Your Birthday At Booking.

What Cabana Types Are Available?

Three Types: Interior Cabanas (Up To 10 Guests), Double Cabana (Up To 20 Guests, Most Popular For Birthdays And Larger Groups), And Rooftop Lounge (Exterior Open-Air, Up To 10 Guests).

How Do I Book A VIP Cabana?

Cabanas Are Booked Online Via Tock. Select Your Cabana Type, Date, And Time — Then Add Any Extras At Checkout. Takes Under 2 Minutes.

Private & Corporate Events

5 Questions

How Many Guests Can THRöW Social DC Accommodate For A Private Event?

Up To 350 Guests Across 11,000 Sq Ft Of Rooftop And Indoor Space. Full Venue Buyouts Are Available.

How Do I Book A Private Event At THRöW Social DC?

Fill Out The Private Events Enquiry Form Or Email Guests@Throwsocialdc.Com. We Respond Within 24 Hours With Package Options And Pricing. No Commitment Required To Inquire.

Is Axe Throwing Available For Private Events?

Yes — Kick Axe DC Is Downstairs In The Same Building And Can Be Incorporated Into Your Event Package. Axe Throwing, Nerf Battles, And The Den Can All Be Combined With The THRöW Social Rooftop Experience.

Does THRöW Social DC Have AV Equipment For Presentations?

Yes — The Main Stage Features A 20-Ft Video Wall With Full HD Display, Professional Sound System, And Lighting Rig. Available For Corporate Presentations, Brand Content, And Live Video Feeds.

Can I Do A Full Venue Buyout For My Event?

Yes — Full Venue Buyouts Are Available. Contact Guests@Throwsocialdc.Com With Your Date And Guest Count For Pricing.
